# Lab 10 — Independent AI Solution-Design Practice

## Purpose

Practise defining a small, dependable AI-enabled system before choosing a vendor, platform or model.

## Assessment boundary

Do not use an assessed bootcamp capstone scenario for this exercise. The goal is to practise the design method without receiving hints about the final projects.

## Template

Copy `templates/ai_solution_one_page_brief.md` into your own working folder. Keep the original blank.

## Choose a neutral practice scenario

- equipment inspection note organiser;
- library-book discovery helper;
- community-event FAQ assistant;
- greenhouse sensor-alert explainer;
- accessibility document checker; or
- another problem unrelated to the assessed capstones.

## Design questions

- Can ordinary rules solve the problem without AI?
- Who is the user and what decision are they making?
- What is the smallest useful output?
- What data is genuinely necessary?
- Which data must not be collected?
- What happens when the system is uncertain?
- What can happen automatically?
- What requires human approval?
- Which metric or test demonstrates value?
- Which log proves that a control ran?
- What is the rollback or safe-stop path?

## Completion criterion

You can explain the problem, data, workflow, control boundaries, evaluation and human responsibility without naming a model or vendor first.

## What to submit

This practice brief is for learning unless your instructor explicitly asks for it. It is not a substitute for the separately issued capstone requirements or submission.
