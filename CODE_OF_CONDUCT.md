# Community Code of Conduct

This repository supports an MMAUG learning environment. Participants and contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, welcome different experience levels, and give constructive technical feedback.

Do not use examples, datasets or exercises to expose personal, confidential or sensitive information. Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory conduct and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

For the community's current governance and conduct information, refer to the official MMAUG channels and website: https://mmaug.com/
